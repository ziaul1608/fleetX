# FleetX — Project Documentation & Academic Artifacts

This directory contains the official academic documentation, specifications, presentations, and research publications for the **FleetX — Intelligent Fleet & Logistics Management System** developed by the Project-Based Learning (PBL) Capstone team at the **Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, Arya College of Engineering & IT, Jaipur**.
